The server pins the actual saved role CV/letter and current applicant-confirmed answers; the master CV cannot silently fill a missing role document. Unicode and line endings are retained in raw UTF-8 fingerprints. Facts, job evidence, destination, document or parent-state changes invalidate use. Late edits, account deletion, fact revocation, pause and original-close/change races are guarded at insertion.
