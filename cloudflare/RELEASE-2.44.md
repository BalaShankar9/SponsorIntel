# Sponsor Intel 2.44 — private discovery evidence and follow-up

The count audit showed that refreshing approved employers did not provide a durable record of new recruiting leads. The owner dashboard now retains LinkedIn job adverts and ordinary posts, employer originals and recruiter leads in a private queue. Every decision preserves the source observation, known dates and their evidence, sponsorship quotation, employer identity, reason, recording method and explicit follow-up.

Canonical LinkedIn IDs prevent repeat URLs from becoming new leads. Accepted links must match an existing current job's approved source, employer, original application URL and quoted wording. The immutable revision captures the exact job text and metadata privately. Later changes, source pauses, lost approval, deadlines and stale jobs invalidate the current match without erasing the earlier judgment or altering the public job. Multiple leads for one role count one linked opportunity. Linking is not publication, proof of causation or a new sponsorship promise.

The hourly business snapshot retains aggregate backlog, overdue, changed-link and distinct linked-job counts, creating normal-priority findings when action is due. The daily Codex sourcing routine is directed to search existing leads, preserve decisions and record bounded new public leads here. Neither workflow acquires a LinkedIn collector, new model allowance or automatic publishing authority.
