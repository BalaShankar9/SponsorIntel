## Delivered

- Seven new public pages: `/routes`, `/routes/business-self-sponsorship`, `/routes/india-to-uk`, `/study`, `/study/uk-costs`, `/immigration-sources` and `/editorial-policy`. They contain 11 selected visa-route comparisons, original guidance links and reviewed dates. They do not decide personal eligibility or promise a low-investment visa. Business guidance distinguishes a genuine sponsored role from company registration and separates employer charges from salary, business capital, personal visa charges and the health surcharge.
- A searchable official student-sponsor directory. The production 5 October snapshot contains 1,303 source rows and 946 provider records, with separate provider types/statuses retained. There are 758 Student and 545 Child Student source rows; these counts overlap at provider level. Cardiff search returned ten matching provider records. A licence is not confirmation of course availability, a CAS offer or immigration success.
- Daily official student-register checks and an owner refresh control. Imports validate the publication, allowed attachment host, schema, source date and size before switching the snapshot. A failed import retains the last successful dataset and exposes the failed check separately. Current and previous snapshots are retained.
- Eight dated fee examples from four universities, with academic year, duration and source attribution. The editable study planner separates total spending from financial evidence and avoids counting the evidence requirement as an extra fee. This is not a complete course catalogue or a cheapest-in-the-UK ranking. Business-charge estimates show the selected sponsor size, duration and applicable exemption assumptions.
- Five trusted publisher/channel starting points. No invented social handles or customer ratings. Eighteen reviewed guidance hashes support change warnings. The source watcher now covers 20 fixed GOV.UK pages and four recent Immigration Rules publications. The live scheduled run at 13:15 UTC checked 24 sources with zero failures; checks do not establish a legal review of every change. The immigration assistant can retrieve the additional route and business sources when relevant.
- Account verification and password recovery through Cloudflare Email Service. Verification expires after one hour; reset links after 30 minutes. Signup requires verification before sign-in. Reset use revokes existing sessions. Requests are bounded by address, IP and app, redirects remain on the current allowed origin and token-bearing pages/auth responses use private caching and no-referrer. Recovery codes remain available.
- Application Studio now includes a source-backed company brief from the current advert, a portfolio preparation plan and a two-week learning plan. A manually entered role produces an honest research checklist. Company briefs do not use AI or transmit a CV, and are not independent company investigations. This is not a portfolio-site publisher or a wholesale port of every HireStack agent.
- CV/letter review tolerates harmless punctuation and line-break differences when validating cited claims, while retaining source-ID and numeric checks. Supplied contact details are preserved deterministically and unresolved date labels are removed. The prompts retain project provenance, actual dates, AI assistance and the distinction between ideas and completed work.
