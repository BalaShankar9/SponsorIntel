# Sponsor Intel 2.39 — evidence-linked vacancy promotion drafts

## Result

Marketing can now consider up to three current vacancy candidates alongside the three sourced guides. A candidate needs explicit offered/conditional employer wording, an approved employer source, a current reviewed Skilled Worker register match and a recent successful check of its exact application page. A licence alone is insufficient. Candidate and source observations expire within 24 hours, with any known closing date shortening that window; a proposed publishing slot must fit before expiry. A short-lived vacancy cannot suppress the durable guide candidates.

The planner chooses one eligible candidate or no post. Vacancy copy is assembled from four fixed facts, including the complete retained sponsorship quotation and the distinction between employer licensing, vacancy wording and applicant eligibility. It is not rewritten by an AI writer. A different-model reviewer receives the complete retained advert and assesses every paragraph. Rejection holds the card without a rewriting attempt. This path uses two model calls within the existing shared daily allowance; guide and welcome paths retain their limits.

Migration 0026 pins public job identifiers, an advert/evidence fingerprint and a checked-link reference to each immutable content version. Review and schedule records recheck the evidence and use database predicates that reject a concurrent feed change, pause or approval withdrawal. The hourly business workflow holds changed proposed/reviewed cards while preserving their text and history. Changed externally scheduled or uncertain cards create a high-priority action; the local workflow cannot cancel a provider job. A later publisher must inspect the original advert and provider queue before scheduling and delivery, or prefer durable guidance. No direct publisher, cancellation adapter or expanded account permission is introduced.

The owner desk shows the bounded opportunity pool, exact employer wording and expiry. Its counts describe sampled candidates only. Existing queue rules remain at most three Facebook/Instagram company posts per rolling seven days and one new schedule per daily run. The new `fb-opportunity` mapping attributes declared campaign actions to the dated vacancy. No private CV, applicant record or customer identifier enters this feature. Employer-link sampling now prefers offered/conditional adverts only when previous check age is tied; source/role rotation and request ceilings are unchanged.
