# 2.98.6 — verify worker-register content before renewing freshness

30 October 2026. The worker-register refresh previously renewed its successful-check timestamp when the official CSV URL stayed the same, without downloading that file. A changed or unavailable file at that URL could therefore leave old records labelled recently checked.

Every attempted check now requires the complete CSV, exact five-column schema, existing body/row/size guards and non-regressing, non-future source date. Every normalized identity, name, town, county, rating, route and Skilled Worker flag is compared with the retained snapshot through bounded indexed pages. Only an exact complete match reuses the snapshot. A new attachment with identical records can reuse it; changed content at the same URL still stages and verifies a complete replacement. Source-row counts and dated provenance come from this actual check, not URL equality. No successful timestamp advances after an unavailable, invalid or truncated source.

A five-minute owned lease and exact prior-metadata comparison guard activation. Failure recording preserves a committed pointer after a lost acknowledgement, cannot overwrite a newer decision, and does not retry. Cleanup requires the current exact metadata and owned unexpired lease. Existing source and customer holds remain unchanged.
