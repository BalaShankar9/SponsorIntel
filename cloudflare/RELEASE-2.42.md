# Sponsor Intel 2.42 — original job dates and qualified search metadata

Greenhouse supplies `first_published`, but ingestion previously discarded it and every advert omitted JobPosting structured data. Source edit dates and our observations cannot establish original publication. The importer now preserves the valid, nonfuture original date separately and displays it alongside source-edit and observation dates. Missing/invalid values remain unknown; Ashby republication dates, RSS dates and our own observations are never substituted.

Current Greenhouse adverts can emit JobPosting with that date, full visible employer text, an application URL and an explicit single reviewed UK city/country. Unsupported, ambiguous, multiple-location and remote descriptions retain their ordinary WebPage/Breadcrumb metadata. This conservative rollout may exclude legitimate remote roles; broader mapping needs source evidence. No salary, deadline, visa eligibility or direct-apply claim is invented. Known deadlines are included. Removal, expiry and staleness remove the object; collections and the fallback hostname never emit it. Server and browser share the rules.
