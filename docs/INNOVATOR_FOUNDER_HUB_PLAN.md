# Innovator Founder hub: standing product scope

Owner scope consolidated 26 October 2026. This expands Sponsor Intel; it does not replace the existing autonomy, sourcing, applications, business identity or learning objectives. The separate Goal API reported usageLimited today; this document records scope without claiming to resume or edit that Goal. Other projects and existing provider/source/model holds remain unchanged.

## Product outcome

Give a prospective founder a coherent journey from understanding the route and developing a commercially credible idea through validation, endorsement preparation, visa preparation and later business milestones. Build useful, polished working increments. Do not describe generic generated ideas as innovative, endorsed or visa-ready. A preparation checklist, model agreement or successful software test cannot decide eligibility or establish endorsement, visa approval or commercial demand.

The earlier private founder conversations informed the journey and emphasis on achievable founder-led preparation. Their private ideas, personal details and generated claims are not public evidence and must not be copied into the hub. Existing ChatGPT project, source conversations and integration Page have already been organised; do not duplicate or relocate them.

## Delivery stages and acceptance

1. **Integrated foundation:** public `/innovator-founder` with mobile navigation, source-dated route essentials, six-stage journey, an editable evidence workbook, conservative adult fee illustration, searchable role-labelled support directory, shortlist comparison and explicit export/restore. Generic static SEO shell; current-sensitive facts load separately and expire. Workbook starts empty and stays in tab memory, with prominent loss warning and export. No AI or account data migration. Accept after source review, privacy/import/expiry/calculation tests, actual browser editing/restoration and narrow-screen/keyboard checks, build, full suite and live verification.
2. **Private saved workspace:** signed-in project progress, evidence attachments/references and milestones with account isolation, export/delete coverage, version/conflict/replay guards, offline recovery and shared-device sign-out behaviour. Do not add an unknown field to the existing career payload: older clients could erase it. Test two accounts, stale saves, malformed imports, deletion and recovery before claiming cloud persistence. Existing tab exports remain portable and do not become account data without consent.
3. **Validation and planning workbench:** structured customer interview evidence, competitor comparison, innovation/viability/scalability reasoning, founder skills and learning gaps, experiment outcomes, honest financial scenarios, business-plan and pitch assembly. Separate facts, hypotheses, assumptions and results. Dates, source links and counter-evidence matter. No automatic invented interviews, traction, IP, skills, funding or outcomes; any AI-assisted proposal needs approved privacy/cost design and independent quality evaluation within unchanged limits.
4. **Verified directory depth:** distinguish currently authorised new-application endorsers, invitation-only programmes, legacy-only bodies, regulated immigration professionals, incubators, accelerators, mentors and finance/support resources. Source each role, regulatory status, contact route, geography, stage, eligibility, fees, availability and last check. Unknown intake/capacity/fees remain unknown. Useful filters, bookmarks and side-by-side comparison must not imply ranking or endorsement. Verify named advisers on their current regulator before publication; an adviser-search portal is not itself an adviser. Initial directory is deliberately selected, not comprehensive. Contacts/outreach, subscriptions and applications require separate authorisation.
5. **Application and later milestones:** evidence checklist and export tied to the current official guidance, endorser-specific requirements, personal eligibility handoff, endorsement validity, document preparation, later contact points and extension/settlement review. Do not promise a fixed zero-to-approval timeline or turn possible settlement into entitlement. No submission, payments, legal representation or personalised immigration decision is authorised by the product build.
6. **Source operations and useful outcomes:** immutable versioned source corrections, review expiry, change detection with human/qualified interpretation where required, broken-link reporting, accessible error/empty states and useful aggregate measurement excluding owners/QA. Existing feedback route receives corrections. Avoid bulk SEO pages and legal social automation. Validate whether users finish useful evidence work; page visits/download requests are not saved files, endorsements or conversions.

## Source and privacy contract

Use current GOV.UK rules and guidance for route/fees/authorised endorsers, current regulator entries for professionals and provider originals for services. Retain checked dates and precise supporting sections. Provider marketing claims are not independent quality ratings. Recheck before spending/applying. Initial sources checked 26 October, review due 2 November; expiry hides the fee result and changes directory status to historical, retaining original links. Do not silently renew dates or hashes. New hub content is an agent-assisted curated explanation, not independent legal review or automatically monitored legal guidance. Existing route-page explanations and baselines are not refreshed by adding a hub link.

No passport, bank statement, CV or sensitive document upload in the initial workbook. Text is local tab memory; no localStorage, cloud save or model calls. Exports are private files under user control. Import only the exact versioned format with bounded strings, known keys and curated directory identifiers; do not render imported HTML, activate arbitrary URLs or merge unknown fields. Confirm replacement of a nonempty workbook. Warn before leaving with changed work. Keep downloads explicit and report requests accurately.

Budgets, source reviews, social cadence, approvals and the stalled business-supervisor hold remain governed by DAILY_OPERATOR.md. This hub adds no automatic external action, service purchase, legal claim update or AI allowance.
