# Reviewed businesses behind sponsor entries

Release 2.88 adds owner-reviewed business relationships. Open **Admin → Sources → Business identities**. A new record starts held and requires selecting an exact entry from the current register. Trading name, website operator, operating organisation, subsidiary and group relationship are distinct choices. Describe the direction and scope of a group relationship in its public evidence summary; shared ownership or a similar name is not a vacancy's employing entity.

Record the business website, optional sourced activity/operating locations/public identifier/careers URL, primary relationship evidence and a public summary. Unknown fields stay blank. Record a separate private source-rights assessment and private decision reason. The evidence observation is deliberately blank initially: supply the actual observation within 72 hours for new records or approvals, not the form-open time. Existing records can be held or withdrawn using their original observation, without inventing a recent source visit. Withdrawal does not require renewing an expired follow-up. Set a follow-up within 30 days. Attribute agent-assisted work honestly; an owner-account save is not independent human validation.

Approval requires fresh register metadata (successful check within two days, source date within seven days), the exact current sponsor ID/name and a complete review. An atomic write rechecks the register snapshot and evidence identity. This is a semantic review by the operator; URL/date validation cannot itself establish that the claimed relationship is true or reuse is permitted. Never approve a missing or contradictory fact to increase coverage.

Every decision appends an immutable revision and an atomic audit entry. A request key identifies exact content and actor, making a lost-acknowledgement retry safe. Replaying an older request returns the latest current state alongside the original saved revision; it does not undo a subsequent hold. Concurrent/stale edits conflict. An exact normalized business name for one sponsor must be reviewed through its existing record, including withdrawn records. Different brands for one legal entity and the same brand for different entities are supported. A record's sponsor identity cannot be reassigned: withdraw and review a separate relationship.

Approved, unexpired relationships appear on the sponsor profile and existing licence-linked job detail, and extend directory name search while preserving city, route, rating and pagination filters. Public responses are not cached. Held, withdrawn, expired, missing or renamed register identities are excluded; an unavailable/stale register hides current business profiles and disables alias expansion. Existing open pages refresh on navigation; this is not a live revocation push to already-rendered browsers. Private notes, rights assessments, actor and request identifiers are not public.

This feature does **not** configure an employer feed, change an existing job/brand-to-licence link, publish a job or change sponsorship wording. Existing employer-link review and advert holds remain separate. A business profile cannot resolve the legal employer of a particular vacancy by itself. No automatic candidate approval, enrichment crawl, Companies House API or new scheduled service is connected.

Due-review counts are in the owner desk. Inspect them in the daily operating routine. Integration into hourly business findings is still open: the existing business monitor is part of the protected research-comparison identity and was left unchanged. Research/application source fingerprints and all existing evaluations remain untouched. Future integration needs its own version review; never regenerate a protected hash just to make tests pass.

Use only the current public business information, without private contacts, individual managers, ratings/logo or an endorsement claim.

[CQC terms](https://www.cqc.org.uk/about-us/our-policies/terms-conditions), inspected 20 October, permit CQC copyrighted public material under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), excluding identified third-party material and separate logo rights. Attribute CQC and link its original record. The Numan website's separate terms were not treated as commercial content-reuse permission; this pilot uses CQC evidence and a normal website link. This does not settle reuse rights for a different source/feed.
