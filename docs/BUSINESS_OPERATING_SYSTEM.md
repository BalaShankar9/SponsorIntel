Withdrawing the report removes its searchable article and sitemap entry, supersedes unsent drafts and pauses future publication. It does not erase the retained evidence versions.

## Publishing and search quality

The first report has one stable address: `/insights/uk-sponsorship-jobs-report`. Each publication stores the true measurement time, a previous snapshot comparison when available and its contributing boards. Refresh the useful report rather than multiplying near-identical weekly URLs. The visible report and Article metadata agree. Unavailable or withdrawn articles return 404/410 with noindex. The insight hub, live article, feed and job pages are crawlable without JavaScript.

Do not call catalogue growth overall UK market growth, event counts unique visitors, a licence a sponsorship offer, positive advert wording personal eligibility, or unpublished drafts live social posts. Source health is not independently measured classification accuracy. Avoid duplicate SEO pages, purchased links, fake reviews, arbitrary date changes and unsupported “number one” claims. Google's [generative-AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) asks for useful added value; [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) cover scaled content abuse.

Routine editorial expansion should start with original advert-quality audits, transparent source-coverage improvements and tested product tutorials. Immigration changes require exact official sources, dates, affected audiences, source-version checks and qualified review where interpretation or personal circumstances matter. Do not feed private CVs, legal questions, email addresses or user feedback into a public article or social draft.
