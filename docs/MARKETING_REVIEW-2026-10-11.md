## Honest capability assessment

The implementation is tailored to Sponsor Intel: a bounded planner/writer, a different-model critic, versioned evidence, exact destinations, shared limits and delivery reconciliation. Those controls are useful. The current strategy is narrow (three fixed guide topics and a small eligible vacancy pool), model review has a real held failure, and direct cloud publishing is not connected. A daily desktop routine and provider scheduler remain part of delivery. There is no proven audience-learning loop, repeatable conversion, sales engine or independently accepted content-quality benchmark. Calling this the world's best team would overstate the evidence.
