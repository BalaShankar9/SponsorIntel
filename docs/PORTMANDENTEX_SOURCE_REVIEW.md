## Collection behavior

Each scheduled pass reserves 50 requests within the existing 150/run and 500/day limits, scans at most five catalogue pages, retrieves at most 40 changed/missing/stale descriptions sequentially with at least 200 ms between request starts, then scans the complete catalogue again. Every record must retain its public identity, published sections and original destination. Custom administrative fields and creator details are excluded from the cache and public output, including non-advertised pay fields.

Descriptions and source progress persist privately under the existing source lease. A failed, incomplete, changed, oversized or uncertain batch cannot be labelled a successful publication. No partial catalogue becomes public. A completed consistent set goes through existing evidence checks, source-drop review, lease fencing, atomic job/receipt writes, pause controls and replay protection. Resuming a completed task performs no second fetch.

Unchanged versions reuse their original retrieval time for at most seven days. Catalogue observation, text retrieval and source-supplied release dates remain distinct on the public detail page. Changed or stale entries are refreshed; missing current roles retire only with a complete accepted publication. Existing three-day public freshness and any explicit deadline limits still apply while a new batch is collecting. Seven passes may be needed for an unchanged 249-entry starting catalogue; changing inventory, failures, exclusions and existing limits can alter that. Never promise an initial completion time or increase the public count from the inventory alone.

The owner panel shows gathered/total and the current pass count. Recent initial progress gets a normal coordination finding; failed, future-dated, stale or prolonged bootstrap still needs attention.
